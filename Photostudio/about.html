Hi I'm Ammi!


I am a portrait and wedding photographer based in Bozeman, Montana. I have been photographing families since 2010. To make the magic happen,I wear many hats! On any given day, I get to be a goofball with the kiddos, cat wrangler, fashion consultant, dog whisperer, interior decorator, and back to goofball.
